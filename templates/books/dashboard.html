About Our Library

Our Library Management System provides an easy and organized way to manage books, students, teachers, and book borrowing. It helps users find books, track issued books, monitor due dates, and maintain library records efficiently.

Our goal is to encourage reading, support learning, and make library management simple, fast, and convenient.
